# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Company managers run daily service operations across incoming requests, active orders, schedules, customers, services, and team workload.
- Company employees review assigned work and coordinate delivery through order and schedule views.
- Customers discover services, submit requests, and track their own requests and orders.
- Super administrators govern provider-company approval and account status.

## Product Purpose

Drixal is a multi-tenant service management platform that connects service discovery, customer requests, provider review, order conversion, assignment, scheduling, and customer tracking. Success means each role can understand current operational state and complete its next task with minimal navigation and ambiguity.

## Positioning

Drixal carries a service request from public discovery through tenant-controlled approval, conversion, assignment, and private customer tracking in one role-aware workflow.

## Operating Context

The primary operating scene is a company manager repeatedly scanning new demand, active work, schedule pressure, customer context, and exceptions throughout the workday. Structured lists, tables, statuses, filters, and contextual actions are core working materials. Public marketplace and authentication surfaces support entry into this operational system.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Nuxt 4, Vue, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Nuxt UI, MongoDB, and Mongoose are the established stack.
- Four protected workspaces serve super administrators, company administrators, company employees, and customers.
- Role permissions, tenant isolation, and private customer records must remain intact.
- The implemented lifecycle includes service publication, customer requests, approval or rejection, order conversion, assignment, scheduling, and company governance.
- Payments, notifications, reviews, subscriptions, and external integrations are planned, not current product claims.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Drixal.
- The official brand blue is `#2546F0`.
- The product uses Cairo throughout and treats Arabic RTL and English LTR experiences as equally important.
- The interface follows an enterprise, IBM Carbon-inspired system: structured, restrained, operational, modern, and free of decorative AI-interface tropes.

## Evidence on Hand

- Implemented workflows and demo roles are documented in `README.md`.
- Existing product tokens and interaction rules are documented in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- Seeded demo data supports realistic product demonstrations; it must not be presented as production business evidence.
- No testimonials, customer logos, benchmark claims, or commercial performance evidence are currently established.

## Product Principles

- Put the next operational decision ahead of decorative metrics.
- Preserve role clarity, tenant boundaries, and trustworthy status communication.
- Keep structured business data scannable and actions contextual.
- Make Arabic and English equally complete rather than treating either direction as a fallback.
- Prefer simple, consistent workflows over feature-shaped visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic labels and table headers, and non-color status cues are required. Bidirectional layout must preserve hierarchy, icon meaning, and action order in both Arabic and English.
